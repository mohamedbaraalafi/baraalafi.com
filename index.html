About

Who am I?

Master's student in Analysis and Policy in Economics (APE) at the Paris School of Economics. My master thesis, supervised by Tobias Broer, looks at monetary policy shocks in the euro area and how their effects differ between core and periphery countries.

My interests lie in macroeconomic, fiscal and sovereign analysis: how monetary policy passes through to public finances, debt affordability, fiscal stress, sovereign spreads and country risk. My toolkit is time-series and panel econometrics, local projections, structural VARs and quantitative macro models.

Research

Areas of interest

Three axes where monetary policy, public finances and sovereign risk meet: the questions central banks, rating agencies and sovereign advisers work on.

01

Monetary–Fiscal Interaction

How each dimension of ECB policy (policy rate, forward guidance, asset purchases, transmission protection) passes through to sovereign yields, spreads, interest bills and primary balances, and why core and periphery respond differently.

02

Sovereign Risk & Fiscal Sustainability

Debt dynamics and affordability, fiscal-stress indicators, ratings and country-risk assessment, fiscal forecasts and stress scenarios for euro-area and emerging-market sovereigns.

03

Macro-Financial Analysis & Public Finance

Business cycles and open-economy macro, capital flows, taxation and tax incidence, subsidies and state-owned enterprises, with applications to the MENA region.

Current work · Master thesis (in progress)

Monetary Policy Shocks in the Euro Area: Effects on Core and Periphery

How ECB monetary policy shocks transmit to sovereign bond markets, macroeconomic outcomes and public finances, and why the response differs between core and periphery countries of the euro area.

Paris School of Economics · Supervised by Tobias Broer · 2026–2027
