About

Who am I?

Master's student in Analysis and Policy in Economics at the Paris School of Economics, with a background in applied economics, quantitative analysis and empirical research, working with economic data, econometric methods and causal inference tools to answer policy-relevant questions.

My research centers on macroeconomics and public policy evaluation, applying rigorous econometric methods to questions that matter for institutions and firms alike, from policy design to strategic decision-making. I aim to produce research at the intersection of analysis and real-world impact.

Areas of interest

Three axes where rigorous economic analysis meets policy and strategy.

01

Applied Econometrics & Policy Evaluation

Causal inference (IV, DiD, RDD, Synthetic Control) applied to public-policy questions, the core toolkit behind economic consulting and policy evaluation.

02

Macroeconomics & Country Risk

Macro and sovereign-risk analysis, inflation, fiscal sustainability, country risk, public finance, regulation.

03

Productivity, Development, Growth & Consulting

Long-run productivity, development and growth strategy at the macro, state and firm level, with economic consulting and policy analysis.
