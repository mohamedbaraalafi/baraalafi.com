About

Who am I?

I am a young economist, currently studying economics at the Paris School of Economics. My research focuses on economic growth, institutions and development, with a particular interest in emerging economies.

Passionate about rigorous academic research and its applications to public policy, I aim to produce work that informs decision-making as much as it advances knowledge.

“The difficulty lies not so much in developing new ideas as in escaping from old ones.”
John Maynard Keynes

Research

Areas of interest

My work is organized around three main axes, at the crossroads of theory and public policy.

01

Economic Growth & Institutions

The role of institutions in long-run economic performance, with a focus on emerging economies.

02

Development & Productivity

Trade openness, human capital and firm productivity in developing countries.

03

Applied Public Policy

Policy evaluation and administrative data, with particular attention to developing countries.
